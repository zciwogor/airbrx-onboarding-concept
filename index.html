Questions
How it works
Do I replace my warehouse?

No. Think of AiRBRX as short-term memory and your warehouse as long-term memory. Anything AiRBRX doesn't remember goes straight to your warehouse, exactly as it does today.

How do I start, and how do I leave?

Change one hostname in your BI tool, agent or code. To leave, change it back. No migration, no rewritten queries.

How is this different from my warehouse's own result cache?

A warehouse's built-in result cache only helps when the exact same query text comes back, and it clears easily as data changes. AiRBRX keeps answers in a form that also covers related questions, and only clears what a change actually affects.

How does it remember related questions?

It keeps answers as a data cube. If it has beer sales by taproom and day around Mount Hood, it can answer "sales at Government Camp last week" or "sales by taproom" without asking your warehouse again. More on airbrx.ai

Which answers does it remember?

The ones your tools and agents actually ask for. You can add your own rules, and AiRBRX's built-in AI reviews your query history and logs to suggest new ones.

Can a stale number ever reach a dashboard?

Not if you set the rules for it. Freshness is yours to control, down to the millisecond: time limits, table changes, PII rules, or anything you can write in SQL. Writes always clear the answers they affect.

Will it slow down questions it doesn't remember?

No noticeable delay. The gateway adds less than a tenth of a millisecond.

What happens if AiRBRX goes down?

AiRBRX to confirm whether queries pass straight through to your warehouse.

AI agents
Does it help with AI agents?

Most of all. Agents ask the same questions over and over, often worded differently. AiRBRX answers them from memory instead of re-running them on your warehouse.

Can I see what my AI agents are querying?

Yes. The audit log records every statement from every agent, tool and person, at the transaction level.

What stops a rogue agent?

AiRBRX blocks agents that keep guessing credentials and stops runaway query storms before they run up your bill.

Fit
Which platforms and tools work?

Snowflake, Databricks and Postgres. Anything that connects with a hostname works: Power BI, Tableau, dbt, Python, JDBC and AI agents.

Does it help with streaming data?

Not much. AiRBRX is built for analytics: dashboards, reports and AI agents asking questions of your data.

Can it run in our own cloud?

Yes, for larger accounts. AiRBRX can run inside your own AWS environment, so your data never leaves it.

What's next on the roadmap?

Routing to cheaper engines like DuckDB when your data sits in open files, one view of AI token and warehouse spend, and hosted Databricks with AiRBRX in front.
